Why I built Working AI

I’m curious about what AI can do when the work involves real analysis and judgement. Working AI is where I share what I’m learning through personal experiments and research.

I start with Treasury and finance because that’s the work I know. The aim is to make what I learn useful to others: explain the approach, show the evidence and be clear about its limits.

Some guides draw on tools I’ve used; others examine published research. I make that distinction clear, so you can judge what applies to your own work.
