Meaning comes first

Write Plain gives your AI tool a set of writing instructions. Use it to rewrite text or explain a topic clearly.

  • Say exactly what you mean. Choose familiar words when they express the meaning precisely. Keep necessary technical terms.
  • Make the logic easy to follow. Prefer short sentences, active voice and consistent terminology. Keep conditions and logical links.
  • Remove unnecessary words. Remove filler and repetition. Keep necessary details and qualifications. Keep the author’s tone when it carries meaning.
  • Write naturally. Use correct grammar. Relax a rule if it makes the prose awkward, unclear or less precise.
Keep the intended action, certainty, obligation, timing and scope. Do not turn “reconsider” into “change” or “needs to” into “must”.

Where the rules come from

We wrote the instructions and prepared this skill. The writing principles come from the sources below.

The Economist and George Orwell

Write Plain draws on The Economist’s advice to think clearly, choose familiar words and remove unnecessary language. The Economist passage presents Orwell’s six rules. Orwell wrote them in his 1946 essay, Politics and the English Language.

The Economist’s writing guide · Orwell’s original essay

ASD-STE100

Write Plain adapts selected principles from ASD-STE100. These cover short sentences, active voice, consistent terms and clear instructions. The skill keeps necessary technical terms and allows flexibility. It does not apply the full standard or claim formal compliance.

Write Plain draws on Karpathy’s suggestion to soften ASD-STE100 when asking AI for readable explanations. He suggested this approach; he did not write this skill.

How to use it

  1. Give your AI tool the skill. Copy the full skill below into your AI conversation. Or download it and follow your tool’s instructions for adding skills.
  2. Add your text and tell the AI what to do. Name the intended reader if that affects word choice or technical detail.
  3. Check the rewrite. Compare it with your original. Check actions, certainty, obligations, timing, scope and conclusions.

A request to try

Use Write Plain to rewrite the text below for [reader]. Preserve its meaning and return only the finished text.

To write an explanation, ask: “Use Write Plain to explain [topic] for [reader].”

Results vary by model and context. The skill guides the writing. It cannot guarantee accuracy or improvement.

Before and after

We revised these three examples and checked them against the original paragraphs. They are edited examples, not unchanged results from model tests. They do not show how the skill performs across models or other writing.

01 / Business update

Revised example

Before

Following discussions with the relevant teams, we have reached the view that the proposed launch date may need to be reconsidered. Although most of the preparatory work has been completed, a number of outstanding issues remain in relation to customer support and the approval process. It would therefore be helpful to review these matters before making a final decision on whether to proceed as planned.

With Write Plain

After discussions with the relevant teams, we think the proposed launch date may need to be reconsidered. Most preparatory work is complete, but some issues with customer support and the approval process remain unresolved. It would therefore be helpful to review these issues before making a final decision on whether to proceed as planned.

The rewrite removes filler and links completed work with unresolved issues. It keeps “reconsidered” and “would be helpful” to preserve the action and tone.

02 / Treasury analysis

Revised example

Before

The reduction in the liquidity ratio appears to have been driven primarily by an increase in short-term wholesale funding maturities, rather than by a deterioration in the underlying deposit base. However, the position could become more challenging if a higher proportion of corporate deposits were to leave the bank than is currently assumed. Treasury should therefore assess whether the existing liquidity buffer remains sufficient under a more severe outflow scenario.

With Write Plain

The liquidity ratio appears to have fallen mainly because of an increase in short-term wholesale funding maturities. The fall does not appear to reflect a weaker underlying deposit base. However, if a higher proportion of corporate deposits leaves the bank than currently assumed, the position could become more difficult. Treasury should therefore assess whether the existing liquidity buffer remains sufficient under a more severe outflow scenario.

The rewrite separates the main cause from the deposit assessment. It keeps the uncertainty, the proportion of deposits and the recommendation to assess the buffer.

03 / Everyday instructions

Revised example

Before

Before you submit the application, make sure that all the required documents have been attached and that the information you have entered is consistent with the details shown on your identification. If you realise after submission that something is incorrect, you should contact the support team as soon as possible, since changes may be more difficult to make once the review process has started.

With Write Plain

Before you submit the application, make sure that you have attached all required documents. Make sure that the information you entered matches the details on your identification. If you find an error after submission, you should contact the support team as soon as possible. This is because changes may be harder to make once the review process has started.

The rewrite separates the checks into steps. It keeps “should”, “as soon as possible” and the warning about changes after the review starts.
